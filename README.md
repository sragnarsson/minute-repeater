# Minute Repeater Nº128

A turnable 3D anatomy of a Blancpain Villeret Répétition Minutes (ref. 0033, calibre 33, movement Nº128): the case, the movement traced from video of the watch, the layers taken apart, how it keeps time, and how the repeater counts and rings the hours, quarters and minutes.

Live page: https://sragnarsson.github.io/minute-repeater/

Everything is in `index.html` (three.js r128 from cdnjs, fonts from Google Fonts). Parts seen in the owner's videos are traced; the dial-side striking work and the going train under the bridges aren't visible and are drawn as standard minute-repeater construction. The page marks which is which.
